# Seller research and pilot workflow

The first customer-development loop is public research plus human-approved drafts. It is not mass
messaging, contact scraping, fake account creation, or automated social-media outreach.

## Tool access

The Codex workspace currently has public Etsy listing search, GitHub, web research, and PostHog access.
Etsy search does not provide a seller-messaging channel. If outreach is needed, connect these apps from
the Codex app UI with least privilege:

- Gmail or Outlook with draft-only access;
- Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, or HubSpot for the lead ledger;
- Calendar only if a seller asks for a call.

Do not paste OAuth tokens or API keys into chat. Sending a message remains an explicit user-approved
action. If no mail or CRM connector is available, export a CSV of drafts and send them manually.

## Qualification loop

1. Research 20 public sellers with personalized keychain listings and evidence of active demand.
2. Record shop URL, market/language, product fit, public demand evidence, contact channel, hypothesis,
   status, last contact, and opt-out state.
3. Shortlist 5–10 sellers and draft a specific message for each.
4. Offer a free concierge batch of 5–25 orders in exchange for workflow feedback.
5. Treat only opt-in signups as real users; never populate production with synthetic accounts.
6. Continue pricing experiments only after three completed pilots and at least one explicit paid
   commitment.

The repository remains MIT-licensed while this evidence is collected. The initial paid offer is the
managed hosted workspace, batch workflow, and support—not a closed-source fork or an unvalidated fixed
subscription price.
