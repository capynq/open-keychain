# Code of conduct

Open Keychain is committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate of different experiences and backgrounds.
- Give and receive feedback about code, geometry, documentation, and product decisions professionally.
- Assume good intent while addressing problems directly and clearly.
- Respect project maintainers, contributors, users, and the licenses of third-party work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Attempts to evade moderation or retaliate against a reporter are also prohibited.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the [capynq GitHub profile](https://github.com/capynq). Do not open a public issue containing private or sensitive details. The maintainer will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy where practical, and take proportionate action, including removing content or restricting participation.

This policy applies to project spaces and to interactions where someone is representing Open Keychain or materially affecting its community.
